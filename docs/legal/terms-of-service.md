## 2. What SEctOr does

SEctOr checks how visible an organization's website is to search engines and AI assistants, and helps fix what it finds. Depending on your plan and what is turned on for your organization, this includes:

- a free audit score for any website address,
- saved audits and a list of what to fix,
- fixes applied to a connected WordPress site through the SEctOr companion plugin, plus written steps for fixes we cannot apply,
- full reports of up to 20 pages of your own website (paid plans),
- Google Ad Grants compliance alerts (only for organizations where we have turned it on, and only once Google gives us API access),
- brand voice, consent records and AI-assisted content drafts (only when the AI provider is turned on).

Some features are not available yet. When a feature is not available, the service says so.

## 3. The audit score is our own method

The audit score is worked out by SEctOr's own checks, such as whether your site has structured data, whether AI crawlers are allowed to read it, and how headings are arranged. It is not a Google ranking, a Google score, or a score from any AI provider. Search engines and AI assistants do not publish how they choose results, and they can change at any time.

The free audit reads the raw page, not what appears after scripts run, so sites that build their pages with JavaScript can score lower than they should.
