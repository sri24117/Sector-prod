# Surface brief: apps/web (all routes)

Mode: Operate (task completion), except `/` which is the free-audit funnel and still operates rather than sells: the result is the persuasion.
Scope: restyle every existing route to the binding design system `docs/design/design-system.pdf`. Redesign of the visual layer only; product truth, copy intent, routes, API calls and behaviour are preserved.
Audience/job: generalist NGO comms staff, often on a phone, late, deciding whether to trust SEctOr with credentials.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A careful colleague's working tool, not a vendor's pitch. The score appearing is the hero; the page refuses the centred SaaS hero card, card grids, gradients and alarm-red failure states.
OWN-WORLD: Paper #F6F7F3 ground, ink #161B17 text, ink-muted #5B6058, hairline #E2E4DC. One accent, pine #1F4D3B, spent only on primary actions/links and "passed". Ochre #9C6B1F for "needs attention"; true red #B3261E reserved, unused. Newsreader for the score and page headlines only; Public Sans for everything else. 6px radius, no shadows, hairlines instead of boxes, 4/8/12/16/24/32/48/64 spacing.
STORY: Visitor pastes a URL, watches a number count up, reads a plain-language list of what passed and what needs attention, and each failing item offers its own fix inline. Signed-in staff find Audits, Ad Grants and Content in one plain top nav.
FIRST VIEWPORT: `/`: left-aligned Newsreader headline, one sentence of explanation, URL field plus "Run free audit" in pine, all on paper. After a run, the 72px Newsreader score sits directly on paper, left-aligned, with "out of 100" and a pine underline drawing in sync with the 600ms count-up.
FORM: pinned by the design-system PDF (no form-selection seed; the brief fixes the world).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
